# Beat Bank 0.1.7

- Adds transpose: in the Pattern view, the Left/Right arrows shift every note
  Beat Bank sends down/up one semitone, from -24 to +24 (shown as `TR±n`).
  Notes are not wrapped into the 16 pads; notes beyond MIDI 0-127 are dropped.
  Transpose is not saved with the slot and starts at 0 on load.
- Slows knobs 1-7 (pad/note and swing) to one value step per 6 detents, so
  small nudges no longer jump pads or swing.

Requires Schwung 1.0.0 or newer.
